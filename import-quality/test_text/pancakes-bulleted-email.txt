Fluffy Pancakes

Yield: 12 pancakes
Total time: 25 minutes

INGREDIENTS:
• 2 cups flour
• 2 tbsp sugar
• 1 tbsp baking powder
• 1/2 tsp salt
• 1 3/4 cups milk
• 2 eggs
• 1/4 cup melted butter

METHOD:
Whisk the dry ingredients together.
Whisk the milk, eggs and butter, then stir into the dry ingredients.
Cook 1/4 cup at a time on a hot griddle until golden on both sides.
