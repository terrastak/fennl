Grandma's Banana Bread

Serves 8
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 1 hour

The loaf we make every Sunday.

Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 1/2 cups flour

Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a loaf pan.
2. Mash the bananas and stir in the butter, sugar, egg and vanilla.
3. Fold in the baking soda and flour until just combined.
4. Bake for 1 hour, until a toothpick comes out clean.

Notes
Keeps three days wrapped.
