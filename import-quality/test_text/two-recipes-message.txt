Okay here are the two things for Saturday!

QUICK VINAIGRETTE
3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, pinch of salt. Shake it all in a jar.

LEMON RICE
Rinse 1 cup of rice. Cook with 2 cups of water and a pinch of salt for 15 minutes. Fluff, then stir in the zest and juice of 1 lemon and 2 tbsp butter.
