# Roasted Tomato Soup

Makes 6 servings

## INSTRUCTIONS:
Roast the tomatoes and onion at 400°F for 40 minutes.
Blend with the stock until smooth.
Stir in the cream and season to taste.

## INGREDIENTS:
**For the soup:**
2 lb tomatoes, halved
1 onion, quartered
3 cups vegetable stock
**To finish:**
1/2 cup cream
salt and pepper
